# Product

**User.** A person deciding what to type into an AI chat app: a professional pasting client material, a parent checking an app a teenager uses, a journalist or researcher comparing providers, a privacy-conscious buyer at a small company.

**Painful job.** "Does this app train on my chats, who can read them, how long are they kept, and how do I turn it off?" The answers are spread across privacy policies, help-center articles and settings pages that change without notice, written differently by each company.

**Promise.** One page per app with seven plain answers, each backed by the company's own words and a link to them, plus the exact opt-out steps. Re-checked every week; changes are logged with dates.

**Wedge.** Search ("does Gemini train on my data", "how to stop ChatGPT using my chats", "is DeepSeek data stored in China") and AI answer engines that cite quoted, dated sources. The change feed and embeddable score badges give repeat visits and links.

**Why now.** Providers keep changing these terms. Between this project's first read (10 Nov 2026) and launch (22 Nov 2026), Google renamed Gemini's activity setting, split training into its own default-on setting, and lengthened no-history retention from 72 hours to about two months; OpenAI and Perplexity rewrote their opt-out pages. A static article goes stale in weeks; a scorecard that re-verifies its quotes doesn't.

**Activation metric.** A visitor follows a quote to the company's own document (`source-click`), or copies opt-out steps. Instrumented; counting turns on with the GoatCounter key (OPERATIONS.md, key 3).

**First revenue event.** Not on Pages (non-commercial). The marked slot is a support link; the plausible paid product is a change-alert or monitoring feed for compliance and procurement teams, hosted elsewhere (OPERATIONS.md, key 2).

**What v1 deliberately won't do.** Test the apps, accuse anyone, assess legal compliance, certify anything, rank companies by "trust", cover enterprise contracts, or read pages that robots.txt closes to automated readers.
